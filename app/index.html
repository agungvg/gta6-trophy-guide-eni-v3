The road to the platinum

Three stages, about 200 hours. Play the story first, then clean up the checklist, then mop up strangers and stragglers. Everything you do before the ending already counts toward the 100% checklist — it just is not viewable until after the story.

Save discipline: there is no mission replay. Keep a separate manual save at the start and end of every chapter (10 saves), then rotate the remaining 5 for free-roam cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your platinum save.
